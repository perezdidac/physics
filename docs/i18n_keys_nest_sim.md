# i18n keys created by the NEST-SIM agent (El Bolsó: nest, cold, pole, upgrades, the forestry brigade)

Format: `KEY | Catalan original | English proposal`. `%s` is a placeholder; the sim formats with `tx(KEY, args)`.
`FT_*` are floating texts emitted by the sim, `DMG_*` damage labels (shown as `FT_DAMAGE_LABEL`: "label -N"; `FT_COLD` is used as a label too),
`BOSS_INTENT_*` the intent texts of the boss, `UPG_*` the hub upgrade names and descriptions (the hub screen of NEST-VIEW shows them).
The level-card texts `LVL_NEST_1..4_*` and `LVL_NEST_BOSS_*` (TITLE, INTRO, GOAL, TIP) belong to the view agent.
