# i18n keys created by the PLAT2-SIM agent

Format: `KEY | Catalan | English`. Floating texts shown over the pupa or the moth by `PlatformSim.float_text` in the DIG and FLY modes.

KEY | Catalan | English
--- | --- | ---
FT_PLAT2_DROWN | T'ofegues! | Drowning!

The other sim texts of the two stages reuse the egg ones: sap `FT_PLAT_NUTRIENT`, checkpoint `FT_PLAT_CHECKPOINT`, every hit (stone, beak, ant, lamp, trap, nightjar, bat) `FT_PLAT_HURT`.
The level-card texts `LVL_PUPA_1..3_*`, `LVL_PUPA_BOSS_*`, `LVL_MOTH_1..3_*` and `LVL_MOTH_BOSS_*` (TITLE, INTRO, GOAL, TIP) belong to the story and view agents; the level files only reference the keys.
The boss bars ("Metamorfosi en N s" / "Eggs in N s" and similar) belong to the view agent.
