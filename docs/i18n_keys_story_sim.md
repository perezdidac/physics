# i18n keys created by the SIM agent for the story mode

Format: `KEY | Catalan original | English proposal`. `%s` is a placeholder; the sim formats with `tr(KEY) % args`. Egg and ant names are proper nouns (level data) and stay untranslated.
`FT_*` are floating texts emitted by the sim, `DMG_*` are the damage labels (shown as `FT_DAMAGE_LABEL`: "label -N"), `WPN_*` are weapon names.
