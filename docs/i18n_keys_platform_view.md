# Platformer view texts

Texts of the egg-stage platformer screen (`platform/`) and its results lines (`ui/story/`). Format: `KEY | Catalan | English`.

| KEY | Catalan | English |
|---|---|---|
| `PLAT_PAUSE_TITLE` | Pausa | Paused |
| `PLAT_RESUME` | Continua | Resume |
| `PLAT_EXIT` | Surt al mapa | Back to the map |
| `PLAT_JUMP` | SALTA | JUMP |
| `PLAT_HELP_KEYS` | Fletxes o A/D per moure't · Espai per saltar | Arrows or A/D to move · Space to jump |
| `PLAT_HELP_TOUCH` | Fletxes per moure't · SALTA per saltar | Arrows to move · JUMP to jump |
| `PLAT_BANNER_WON` | Quin solet més bo! | What lovely sunshine! |
| `PLAT_BANNER_LOST` | Torna-ho a provar, Perleta! | Try again, Perleta! |
| `PLAT_NO_WEAPONS` | Cap: salta i amaga't! | None: jump and hide! |
| `PLAT_RESULT_NUTRIENTS` | Gotes de saba: %d de %d | Sap drops: %d of %d |
| `PLAT_RESULT_HEARTS` | Cors que et queden: %d | Hearts left: %d |
| `PLAT_REASON_REACHED_GOAL` | Has arribat al racó de sol. Quina escalforeta! | You reached the sunny spot. How cosy and warm! |
| `PLAT_REASON_LOST` | Les formigues han estat més ràpides. Torna-ho a provar! | The ants were quicker. Try again! |
| `PLAT_BOSS_BAR` | Eclosió en %d s | Hatching in %d s |
| `PLAT_BOSS_INTRO` | La Vespa! | The wasp! |
| `PLAT_BANNER_HATCHED` | Perleta ha trencat la closca! | Perleta has cracked her shell! |
| `PLAT_REASON_HATCHED` | Has aguantat fins al final i la closca s'ha trencat. Benvinguda al món, erugueta! | You held out to the end and your shell cracked. Welcome to the world, little caterpillar! |
| `PLAT_ENEMY_WASP` | Vespa paràsita | Parasitoid wasp |
