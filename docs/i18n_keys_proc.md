Format: `KEY | Catalan original | English proposal`. `%s` is a placeholder; the sim formats with `tx(KEY, args)`.
`FT_*` are floating texts, `DMG_*` damage labels (shown as "label -N"), `BOSS_INTENT_*` the intent texts of the boss (`%s` = the names of the marked followers).
The level-card texts `LVL_PROC_1..5_*` and `LVL_PROC_BOSS_*` (TITLE, INTRO, GOAL, TIP) belong to the view agent.
