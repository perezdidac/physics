# i18n keys created by the SILK2 agent (silk reward on the results screen, rebalanced economy)

Format: `KEY | Catalan original | English proposal`. `%d` is a number. Used by `ui/story/story_results.gd`: the silk chip of a won level, its
breakdown (win, new stars, boss), or the line of a replay that earned nothing.

KEY | Catalan original | English proposal
--- | --- | ---
STORY_RESULT_SILK_GAIN | +%d de seda | +%d silk
STORY_RESULT_SILK_TOTAL | Ara en tens %d per gastar al Bolsó | You now have %d to spend in El Bolsó
STORY_RESULT_SILK_NONE | Ja tens les estrelles d'aquest nivell: cap seda nova. | You already have this level's stars: no new silk.
STORY_RESULT_SILK_BEST | Rècord: %d ★. Supera'l per guanyar més seda. | Best: %d ★. Beat it to earn more silk.
SILK_PART_WIN | Victòria +%d | Victory +%d
SILK_PART_STARS | Estrelles noves ×%d: +%d | New stars ×%d: +%d
SILK_PART_BOSS | Enemic de l'etapa +%d | Stage boss +%d
